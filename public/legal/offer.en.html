Public offer

for access to the online service «ReeAdvent» · version of 23 November 2026 · This English text is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Ukrainian version, the Ukrainian version prevails.

1. General provisions

  1. This document is a public offer by the individual entrepreneur Radchenko Mykola Andriiovych (the Provider) to conclude a service agreement on the terms below (articles 633 and 641 of the Civil Code of Ukraine).
  2. The agreement is concluded when the order on https://reeadvent.fun is paid (acceptance). By paying, the person who places the order (the Customer) confirms that they have read this offer and the Privacy Policy and accept them in full.
  3. Service means the online application «ReeAdvent» at reeadvent.fun. Calendar means a personalised set of 24 daily windows. Participants are the two persons named by the Customer in the order (their email addresses give access).

2. Subject of the agreement

  1. The Provider gives the Participants access to the Calendar: for 24 days in a row, starting on the date chosen in the order, a new window opens every day containing a question for the partner, a gift idea, a suggested adventure for the couple and a shared activity. The content is selected from the Participants' questionnaires.
  2. The service is informational and entertaining. The Provider offers ideas; buying gifts, visiting venues and other actions are carried out by the Participants independently and at their own expense.
  3. The Calendar is intended for personal, non-commercial use by the two Participants.
  4. Territorial restrictions. The Provider does not provide the service to persons placing an order from the temporarily occupied territories of Ukraine (the Autonomous Republic of Crimea and the city of Sevastopol, and certain areas of the Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson and other regions as listed under the legislation of Ukraine), or from the Russian Federation or the Republic of Belarus. The Provider may refuse an order and, if the restriction becomes known after payment, cancel the order and refund the amount paid in full.

3. Price and payment

  1. The price is shown on the website at the time of ordering: in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH) for orders from Ukraine and in euros (EUR) for orders from other countries. The customer chooses the country in the order form. The Provider is a single-tax payer (group 3, no VAT).
  2. Payment is made by bank card through the Monobank payment service. The amount is always charged in UAH. For orders priced in euros, the UAH amount is calculated at the Monobank rate at the time of ordering and is shown in the order form before payment. If the card is in another currency, the card issuer converts the amount at its own rate and may charge its own fees. The Provider does not receive or store card details.
  3. Payment is deemed made when the bank confirms it.
  4. A promo code may give a discount when ordering. The discount is calculated on the website before payment; promo codes cannot be combined and do not apply retroactively. An order with a 100% discount requires no payment.

4. Provision of the service

  1. After payment is confirmed, an email with a sign-in link and a one-time code (valid for 7 days) is sent to both email addresses given in the order: to the Customer, an order confirmation; to the second Participant, an invitation sent on behalf of the Customer. After that, sign-in is by a one-time link or code sent by email, or by a Google account with the same email address.
  2. Each Participant fills in a questionnaire. The Calendar is built after both Participants have filled in their questionnaires. The questionnaire answers are not available to the other Participant. The questionnaire cannot be changed after the Calendar is built.
  3. Windows open every day at 07:00 in the time zone of the Calendar (the time zone of the Customer when ordering), starting on the chosen date. A window cannot be opened earlier.
  4. Answers to the «question of the day» are visible to the other Participant. The gift one Participant gives is not shown to the other.
  5. The service is deemed provided each day in the scope of the opened window.
  6. The Provider sends the Participants service emails: access, reminders to fill in the questionnaire and about new windows (daily at about 09:01 in the time zone of the Calendar). Reminders can be unsubscribed from via the link in the email; access and refund emails are sent regardless.
  7. After the Calendar ends, the Participants keep access to the history (opened windows, questions and answers) for 12 months, after which the Calendar data is deleted (see the Privacy Policy).
  8. If the Customer did not receive an email, they can contact support (section 10) or request a new link or code on the sign-in page.

5. Rights and obligations of the Customer and Participants

  1. The Customer and the Participants confirm that they are at least 18 years old. Part of the content is intended only for adults.
  2. The Customer guarantees that the data given is accurate and that they have the consent of the second Participant to use their email address and take part in the service.
  3. Participants must not pass sign-in links or codes to third parties.
  4. Participants must not enter personal data of third parties, or unlawful or offensive content, in answers to questions. The other Participant sees the answers to the «question of the day».
  5. Copying, publishing, reselling the content of the Calendar and using automated tools for mass collection of data from the service is prohibited.
  6. Adventures for couples are carried out only by mutual consent and within the comfort of both Participants. Any Participant may decline any task.
  7. Venue ratings. A Participant may, at their own choice, rate recommended venues from 1 to 5. A rating should reflect personal experience and must not be knowingly false or malicious. By giving a rating the Participant grants the Provider a free, non-exclusive right to use it in anonymised, aggregated form (average score and number of ratings) to rank and show recommendations to other users of the service. The Provider may hide a venue or remove ratings that show signs of abuse.

6. Changing order details

Before the Calendar starts (before 07:00 on the chosen start date), the Customer may contact support (section 10) and ask to change the start date or the Participants' email addresses. The Provider considers such requests within the time stated in section 10. After the Calendar starts, the date and addresses are not changed.

7. Refunds

  1. The Customer may withdraw from the agreement and receive a full refund before 07:00 (time zone of the Calendar) on the day the Calendar starts (the chosen start date), by contacting support at the details in section 10.
  2. Money is returned in the same way as it was paid (to the card used, through the payment service), within 10 banking days from the day the Provider approves the request. The time it takes to reach the card may depend on the issuing bank. Orders placed with a 100% promo discount are not refundable, because no money was paid.
  3. After the first window opens, the service is deemed provided with the Customer's consent to its provision on the stated days, and no refund is made, except where access to the service was not provided through the fault of the Provider (a technical fault, or a missing access email that could not be fixed). In such cases the Provider, at the Customer's choice, restores access or refunds in full or pro rata.
  4. This section does not limit the consumer rights provided by the Law of Ukraine «On Consumer Rights Protection», other legislation of Ukraine, or mandatory consumer protection rules of the Customer's country of residence.

8. Liability

  1. The Provider makes efforts to keep the service running without interruption but does not guarantee that there will be no technical failures. In case of a failure the Provider restores access within a reasonable time.
  2. The Provider offers ideas for gifts, venues and activities. Links to shops and maps are search queries; the Provider is not responsible for availability of goods, prices, opening hours and the activity of third-party venues and services, which Participants check themselves.
  3. Venue suggestions are compiled from open map data of OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL licence) and Overture Maps (© Overture Maps Foundation), ratings from users of the service and, where enabled, data from third-party map services. This data may be incomplete, outdated or inaccurate; a venue appearing in a suggestion is not an advertisement or a guarantee of quality or safety, and the Provider does not inspect venues or have commercial relations with them. If the suggestions for the Participants' city are not yet prepared, a link to a map search is shown instead.
  4. Participants assess the safety and appropriateness of suggested actions, including trips, activities and intimate tasks, and are responsible for their own decisions. The service does not replace advice from a doctor or another professional.
  5. The Provider is not liable for failure to perform obligations caused by force majeure, including hostilities, mobilisation, power or communication outages, and failures of hosting or payment providers.
  6. The Provider's total liability is limited to the price of the paid service unless the law provides otherwise.

9. Personal data

The processing of personal data is described in the Privacy Policy. By placing the order, the Customer confirms that they have the right to give the Provider the data of the second Participant for this purpose.

10. Contact and dispute resolution

  1. All requests go to support: support@reeadvent.fun or by phone +38 099 562 66 87. The Provider replies within 2 business days.
  2. Disputes are resolved by negotiation and, failing agreement, in court under the law of Ukraine, without prejudice to the mandatory consumer rights of the Customer under the law of their country of residence. A consumer may also contact a consumer protection authority.

The Provider may change this offer. A new version takes effect when published on the website and does not apply to agreements concluded before that.
