Privacy policy

version of 21 November 2026 · This English text is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Ukrainian version, the Ukrainian version prevails.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is the individual entrepreneur Radchenko Mykola Andriiovych («we»). Contact: support@reeadvent.fun, +38 099 562 66 87. Service: reeadvent.fun.

2. What data we collect

  1. Order data: the names and email addresses of both participants, the country, region and city, the chosen start date, your time zone and language, and payment information (order number, status; we do not receive card details).
  2. Participant questionnaires: gift budget, preferences for gifts, activities and adventures, the chosen adventure level, restrictions (for example alcohol) and car availability. These answers may be intimate in nature. We process them only to select tasks, and only with your consent.
  3. Answers to the «question of the day», which you write to your partner, and your marks of completed tasks.
  4. Venue ratings: if you rate a recommended venue (1–5), we store the rating linked to your account in the calendar, to avoid duplicate votes and let you change it. Other users see only an anonymised average score.
  5. Sign-in with Google: from Google we receive only your verified email address; we cannot see your password or other account data.
  6. Correspondence: emails you send to support@reeadvent.fun and our replies.
  7. Technical data: IP address, device and browser type (in the hosting provider's server logs), a session cookie for signing in, and anti-bot signals (Cloudflare Turnstile) when ordering.

3. Why we process it and on what basis

  1. Performance of the agreement (the offer): to accept payment, create the calendar, send access emails and reminders, provide the service and process refunds.
  2. Consent: processing of questionnaires and answers that may concern intimate life. By submitting a questionnaire you consent to this processing. You can withdraw consent by contacting us (the service may then be stopped).
  3. Legitimate interest and legal obligation: security of the service, accounting of payments, replies to enquiries, enforcing the service's territorial restrictions (based on the country and region you give in the order), and selecting and improving venue recommendations from anonymised ratings.

4. Who sees your data

  • The other participant sees only your answers to the «question of the day» (you write them for your partner). They do not see your questionnaire answers or which gift you receive or give.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Cloudflare (hosting, database, network, incoming mail, Turnstile anti-bot), Resend (sending email), Monobank (payment and refunds), Google (sign-in with Google and font loading: your IP address is passed to Google).
  • Map data. For venue suggestions we use open OpenStreetMap data (Overpass and Nominatim services). They receive only a city name or the coordinates of the city centre, never any personal data. If extended recommendations are enabled, Google Places receives a search query (type of activity and city) but not your names, email addresses or answers. The “Search the map” link opens Google Maps at your own initiative, and Google Maps acts under its own policy.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.
  • Data may be processed on servers outside Ukraine and outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. We choose providers with an adequate level of protection and use the safeguards available to us.
  • We may disclose data only when the law requires it.

5. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in (valid up to 60 days, only for the service). There are no advertising or analytics cookies. The Google sign-in button and the anti-bot check may load their own scripts and technical cookies from the providers (Google, Cloudflare).

6. How long we keep data

Calendar data (questionnaires, answers, history of windows) is kept while the calendar runs and for 12 months after it ends, and is then deleted automatically; the order data is anonymised (names and email addresses are erased, the amount and payment date remain for accounting). Support correspondence is kept for 24 months. The link between venue ratings and your account is deleted together with the calendar (12 months after it ends); the anonymised overall score of a venue remains. Payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires. You can ask for earlier deletion (section 7).

7. Your rights

You have the right to: get information about the processing of your data and a copy of it; ask for correction or deletion; restrict processing; object to processing; receive your data in a portable form; and withdraw consent. To exercise them, write to support@reeadvent.fun. We reply within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your national data protection authority; in Ukraine, to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

8. Data security

We use an encrypted connection (HTTPS), store sign-in tokens and codes only as hashes, limit access to the database and lock a sign-in code after repeated failed attempts. No system is completely secure, so do not pass sign-in links or codes to others.

9. Children

The service is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data of minors. If you learn of such a case, tell us and we will delete the data.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with its date.
